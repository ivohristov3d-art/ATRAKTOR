# ATRAKTOR Messaging Guide v1.0

**Status:** LOCKED  
**Date:** 2026-08-01

## One Sentence

**ATRAKTOR is a platform for orchestrating creative and production processes.**

## Short Description

ATRAKTOR organizes complex creative projects by preserving context, tracking decisions, and coordinating workflows across different tools. Rather than replacing the author, it helps maintain continuity and control across the production process.

## Core Promise

**ATRAKTOR preserves context while you create.**

## Core Messages

- Organization before automation.
- Continuity before speed.
- Context before fragmentation.
- The author remains at the center.
- One coherent logic across many tools.

## Communication Tone

Public communication should be calm, professional, intellectual, clear, and free of exaggerated promises.

## Avoid

Do not present ATRAKTOR as:

- a generator;
- a chatbot;
- a replacement for creators;
- magical automation;
- a system that promises everything.

## Canonical Sentence

**ATRAKTOR does not compete with creative tools. It organizes the way they work together.**
