## Public Visual Rule 001

All public materials that show ATRAKTOR as a product must use real product screenshots or canonical runtime evidence.

The interface must not be reconstructed, redrawn, or replaced with conceptual UI.

Editorial elements may be placed outside the product image, but they must not misrepresent the product.
