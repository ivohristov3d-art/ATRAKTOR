# Early Access

ATRAKTOR is preparing for limited external evaluation.

The first external participants will help validate whether the product can preserve context, organize production, and maintain continuity outside its development environment.

Early testing is intended for creative professionals and small teams whose work includes:

- long-running projects;
- multiple tools and production stages;
- version and decision tracking;
- continuity across assets, shots, scenes, or deliverables;
- approval-dependent workflows.

This is not yet a public release or an open beta.

Participation will begin gradually, with controlled installation, clearly defined test scenarios, and structured feedback.

Interest can currently be expressed through the public repository's Issues section.
