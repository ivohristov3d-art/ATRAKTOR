## Rules

- Use real ATRAKTOR screenshots or canonical runtime evidence.
- Do not reconstruct or redraw the interface.
- Do not add fictional panels, controls, states, or capabilities.
- Do not expose private source code, credentials, personal runtime data, or internal-only architecture.
- Keep editorial typography and framing outside the product screenshot whenever possible.
- Every image must correspond to a real product state or an accepted product principle.
